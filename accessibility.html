Our Commitment

Access Multi-Specialty Medical Clinic, Inc. is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all individuals, including those with disabilities. We continually work to improve the user experience for everyone and to conform to the World Wide Web Consortium’s Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA, consistent with the Americans with Disabilities Act and the California Unruh Civil Rights Act.

Conformance Status

This Website partially conforms to WCAG 2.1 Level AA. “Partially conforms” means that while we have taken significant steps toward accessibility, some parts of the content may not yet fully meet the standard. We are actively working to address known gaps.

Measures We Have Taken

To support accessibility, we have implemented the following across the Website:

  • Descriptive alt text on provider photos and informational images to assist screen-reader users;
  • Visible keyboard focus indicators on links, buttons, and interactive controls;
  • Full keyboard-operable navigation, including the dropdown menus;
  • Form fields paired with properly associated label elements;
  • Verified color-contrast ratios against WCAG Level AA thresholds;
  • ARIA (Accessible Rich Internet Applications) landmarks to improve structural navigation for assistive technologies; and
  • A responsive layout with resizable text and descriptive link text.

Known Limitations

Despite our efforts, some content is provided through third-party services that are outside our direct control and may present accessibility barriers that do not fully match our standards, including:

  • The Microsoft 365 appointment intake form; and
  • The embedded Google Maps location map on our Contact page.

If any third-party feature prevents you from completing a task, please contact us using the details below and we will assist you and provide the same information or service through an alternative means, such as by phone, fax, or mail.

Feedback and Accommodation Requests

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of this Website.
