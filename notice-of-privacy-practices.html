9. Substance Use Disorder (SUD) Records — 42 CFR Part 2

[[PLACEHOLDER — COUNSEL TO CONFIRM APPLICABILITY]] If this practice is a "Part 2 program" (e.g., holds itself out as providing SUD diagnosis, treatment, or referral, per 42 CFR § 2.11), records of SUD patients are subject to 42 CFR Part 2 in addition to HIPAA.
