Payment Terms & Important Policies

💳 Payment Policy

  • Fees are due before or at the time of service, or must accompany your written request
  • Services will not be rendered until payment is received unless prior written arrangements are made
  • These services are not covered by medical insurance
  • Payment is required regardless of third-party reimbursement, case outcome, or external determinations

📝 Service Classification

  • Reports, letters, forms, and documentation are administrative/forensic services, not medical treatment
  • Medical-legal services are independent expert services, not treatment services
  • Services may be withheld until all applicable fees are paid in full

📅 Annual Fee Adjustments

  • Fees may be adjusted annually, generally within the range of 5–7%
  • Adjustments effective January 1 of each calendar year
  • Based on changes in operating costs and inflation
  • Adjustments are modest and consistent with customary medical practice standards

⏰ Cancellations

  • Missed appointments or late cancellations may be subject to additional charges
  • Medical-legal services: Cancellations with fewer than 5 business days' notice may be billed in full
